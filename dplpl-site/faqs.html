FAQs

Questions leaders ask us.

Straight answers on how we work, what we deliver and what stays with your organization after we leave.

Strategy, Innovation & Growth

Turn bold ambition into strategy your enterprise can deliver.

Explore this service line → Ask your own question →

We already have a strategy. Where do you add value?

Most of our strategy work begins with a plan that already exists. We test it against what your processes, data and technology can carry today, then build the execution plan that closes the gap: an organized portfolio of projects with clear owners, measured on results and funded to deliver.

How is D++ different from a traditional strategy firm?

We build the strategy and the execution plan together, and the same team stays to deliver it. Our teams combine strategists with data specialists and engineers, so the plan is shaped from the start by what your enterprise can actually build.

How long does a strategy engagement take?

First engagements are deliberately short: a 6-week corporate strategy review, an 8-week growth diagnostic or a 10-week AI-led transformation assignment. Execution then runs in 2-week sprints, so progress is visible from the first month.

How will we know the strategy is working?

Every engagement starts with an agreed baseline and the number that defines success. We measure progress against that baseline, on results rather than activity, and report it the way your board will judge it.

Can you help us decide where AI fits in our strategy?

Yes. Our AI-led transformation work answers a direct question: how will AI actually drive our growth, efficiency, scale and innovation? We identify where it creates value, sequence the investments, and set the success criteria before anything is built.

Our data isn't ready. Should we hold off on AI?

No, but sequence it. Every model inherits the quality of the data beneath it, so we build the foundation the first use case needs, prove value there, then extend it. A 6-week data strategy and governance engagement shows how complete, accurate and consistent your data is today.

How do you decide where AI is worth deploying?

We agree how success will be measured for each use case before we build. We then test against those measures, including unusual cases and costly errors, and deploy only where the business value is clear.

How do you keep people in control of automated decisions?

Every agent we build has a defined identity, role and access permissions, and every action it takes is recorded. We set limits on what each system may do and define when human approval is required, with a clear handover to a person whenever a case needs review.

Can you work in regulated industries such as healthcare and financial services?

Yes. Much of our experience is in healthcare, from payer to provider. Security, privacy and compliance are built into the architecture from day one, and a 4-week readiness engagement tests whether your data, systems, models and processes would hold up under audit.

Who controls our data?

You do. We define what information each system can use, where it may be processed and who can access it. Your organization retains control over its data and over the work carried out on its behalf.

Can we modernize without disrupting day-to-day operations?

Yes. We modernize in deliberate stages rather than one risky leap, starting with what your current systems do well. The business keeps running throughout.

Where do you start?

With your primary value streams. A 6-week current-state engagement maps how work and value flow through the business, measures performance, and shows where time, cost and quality are lost. That evidence decides what to change first.

Do you work in the cloud, on-premise or both?

Both. We choose infrastructure to match the workload, the data requirements and the operating budget, and we design migrations for the business you are becoming, not only the one you run today.

How do you make releases less risky?

Through automated release pipelines, automated testing alongside human code review, controlled releases and MLOps for AI models. The aim is simple: every release becomes routine rather than a risk.

What happens after go-live?

We put live monitoring in place, so your team knows what is running, how it is performing and what to do when something breaks. Documentation and knowledge transfer mean your people can operate and maintain what we built together.

How do you make sure change sticks after launch?

We design the change with the people who will use it, so it is embraced rather than imposed. Clear roles, decision principles and accountability give every part of the progress a committed owner.

What will our teams be able to do once you leave?

Run, maintain and improve what we built together. We provide the documentation, training, mentoring and knowledge transfer your teams need, and we pass the baton only when your organization is ready to operate on its own.

What is a center of excellence, and do we need one?

A center of excellence gives expertise a permanent home, organized around your products and service lines. It provides shared standards, reusable tools and guidance, so delivery no longer waits on the same few specialists. A 6-week assessment shows whether, and where, you need one.

How do you use design thinking in practice?

We follow its five steps: empathize, define, ideate, prototype and test. In 4-week sprints, we test ideas cheaply with real users before your organization invests in building them at scale.

Where do lean and Six Sigma fit?

We apply them where they pay off, not everywhere. An 8-week diagnostic establishes the current state of a process and shows which parts to simplify, which to standardize and which to automate.

Services

What we do

  1. Where should we compete, and how will we win there? We help leadership teams answer both, then translate the answer into a three-to-five-year strategy and annual plans the business can execute.

  2. Where will the next 20% come from? We find the distinctive data and capabilities that set you apart and build a growth thesis on them, with clear bets and the metrics to judge them.

  3. Most AI programs start with technology. Ours start with the P&L. We identify where AI will drive growth, efficiency and scale, then sequence the investments to deliver it.

  4. Strategy fails when no one owns it. We define who owns each outcome, the mix of skills it will take, and whether your organization is ready to carry the change.

  5. We turn the roadmap into a portfolio of funded projects with clear owners, then work in short sprints to deliver it, measured on results rather than activity.

  1. Can your leaders trust the numbers in front of them? We assess how complete, accurate and consistent your data is, then build the governance that keeps one version of the truth.

  2. Analysis only matters if it changes a decision. We review how your organization uses data to decide, and design the architecture that puts insight at the point of decision.

  3. We identify where AI adds value, agree how success will be measured, and deploy predictive models, language models and automation that hold up in production.

  4. We build agents that complete real business tasks. Each one has a defined role, access permissions and a record of every action, with people approving the decisions that need judgment.

  5. Would your data, systems, models and processes hold up under audit? We build controls into the architecture from day one, so you can scale AI without adding regulatory risk.

  1. What are your primary value streams, and how are they really performing? We map them end to end, measure them, and show where time, cost and quality are lost.

  2. Can your operating model carry the transformation you are planning? We test it before you commit, and close the gaps that would stall the program in its middle months.

  3. We examine how your business processes are tied to the technology you run today, and where those ties will break as demand grows.

  4. We design future-state value streams and the technology beneath them, then build both in stages, so the business keeps running throughout.

  5. Secure cloud and on-premise infrastructure, automated release pipelines and MLOps that make every release routine rather than a risk.

  1. We organize centers of excellence around your products and service lines, giving expertise a lasting home and freeing delivery from depending on a few specialists.

  2. We apply lean and Six Sigma methods where they pay off, redesign the process with the people who run it, and automate what should be automated.

  3. Empathy-led discovery, rapid prototyping and build. We test ideas cheaply with real users before your organization invests in building them at scale.

  4. Technology changes in a release; habits change over months. We train and coach your teams to carry the work forward, and measure adoption, not just delivery.

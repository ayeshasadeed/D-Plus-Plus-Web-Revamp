Featured · Strategy, Innovation & Growth

$102M in organic growth, with attribution the CFO could stand behind.

The challenge

Leadership at a multi-site operator could not attribute volume to its source, or tell which customers were most valuable and most likely to convert.

What we did

We built persona-based predictive models, launched multi-channel acquisition programs, and delivered revenue attribution the CFO validated.

The outcome

A repeatable growth engine, with attribution the CFO trusts enough to build a board plan around.

  • +$102MIncremental revenue in three fiscal quarters
  • +$20MIncremental EBITDA
  • 93%+Confidence in a board-approved $41.3M EBITDA plan

How we measure impact

Every engagement starts with a baseline and ends with a number.

We will not promise a painless transformation. We will promise a clear plan, an honest view of the risks, and results you can audit.

  1. 1Agree the baseline.

    Before work starts, we set the measure that defines success with your leadership team.

  2. 2Measure against it.

    Progress is reported on results, not activity, at every stage.

  3. 3Report it your board's way.

    Revenue, cost, cycle time or capacity, in the terms your board already uses.
